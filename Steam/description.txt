Colors every machine in your factory from red to green by how efficiently it is running.

[b]How it works[/b]
[list]
[*] Each machine is tinted by its efficiency, rounded to the nearest 25%: red (0%), orange, yellow, light green, green (100%).
[*] It uses the same measurement as the game's own efficiency panel in the building side panel, and a machine that stops receiving items gets redder.
[*] Belts, splitters, mergers, lifts and belt ports are not tinted intentionally, only machines.
[*] The tint respects the layer toggle (V): machines on hidden layers are not tinted.
[/list]

[b]Toggle[/b]
[list]
[*] A button in the Visualizations panel, directly under the machine conflict button.
[*] The M key (changeable in the keybinding settings).
[/list]

[b]Requires[/b] Shapez Shifter.
